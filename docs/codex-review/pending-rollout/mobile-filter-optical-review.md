## Findings

1. **[P1] Light selected labels fail normal-text contrast, including the accessibility fallback.** `src/app/globals.css:479` binds the selected foreground to primary green, `:461` sets 0.75rem text, `:489` supplies pale muted fallback, and `:582` supplies pale muted at 0.40 with backdrop support. `:604` restores opaque pale muted for reduced transparency, so that preference still leaves the label below the required contrast. At the default 16px root size these labels are 12px and require 4.5:1 (antislop R-25). The resolved primary approximation #16A34A is 3.30:1 against white and 3.01:1 against #F1F5F9. On a white page, the supported white outer surface and pale inner pill remain light; no increase in outer white tint can solve this. The increased-contrast dark pill at `:624` demonstrates the appropriate direction, but normal and reduced-transparency modes remain affected.

   **Smallest justified fix:** keep both selected foregrounds at `hsl(var(--primary))` and change only the light selected pill's neutral tint. Retain the transparent outer capsule, 8px outer blur, 12px inner blur, geometry and restrained reflections. A dark neutral inner material is needed: this green requires adjacent background relative luminance at most 0.02080. #22252A is 4.66:1 and #242424 is 4.71:1. A black inner tint at alpha 0.86 over worst-case white composites near #242424 and retains some transmission; use this as a conservative initial candidate, then tune from actual pixels while maintaining the luminance bound. Use an opaque passing dark neutral for unsupported backdrop and reduced transparency. Do not preserve light 0.40 as a rule, recolor the selected label, darken the entire outer capsule or claim a pale green-on-white treatment passes. Dark theme has no demonstrated token contrast failure and needs no speculative change. Browser sampling still has to verify label-adjacent backgrounds with rim/highlight compositing and the tested stress backgrounds; the numeric candidate is a starting point, not a rendered certification.

2. **[P2] The supposed perimeter reflection also paints across the capsule interior.** `src/app/globals.css:546` layers a 1%-opaque padding-box fill over a diagonal gradient painted through the entire border-box at `:556`; dark mode repeats this at `:565–575`. `background-clip: border-box` clips at the outer border; it does not remove the padding-box interior. A mostly transparent first layer therefore exposes the diagonal white wash inside the surface, instead of confining it to the specified 1–2px rim. This weakens the clearer center requested in the brief (antislop R-10 purpose/dose).

   **Smallest justified fix:** remove the two full-area background images from the decorative pseudo-element and retain a transparent center with its existing 1px border plus top/bottom inset highlights. A restrained inset rim is explicitly permitted by the brief and avoids mask dependence. Only add side edge accents if rendered comparison shows the simpler perimeter is insufficient. Preserve pointer-events none, stacking above the backdrop and below the controls, theme-specific shadows and preference fallbacks.
